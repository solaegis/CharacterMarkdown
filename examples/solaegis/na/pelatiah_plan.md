# Build Plan - Pelatiah: Emissary of the Solar Dawn (Peak Magicka Templar)

> **Character profile:** [pelatiah.md](pelatiah.md) — Level 50 Male Imperial Templar, CP 1103, @SOLAEGIS (NA). Pronouns: **he/him**.

Pelatiah is an Imperial diplomat-monk of Cyrodilic lineage who broke from the collapsed Imperial bureaucracy to serve as an **Emissary of Light and Solar Reckoning** under the Ebonheart Pact. This plan is **peak Templar**: class identity first, not Uber subclassing. He keeps all three native lines — **Aedric Spear**, **Dawn's Wrath**, and **Restoring Light** — and fights as a Magicka solo overland judge: **Puncturing Sweep** jabs, **Radiant Glory** execute, **Ritual of Retribution** cleanse, **Channeled Focus** rune.

Live Pelatiah is already on that Trinity (no subclass) at **CP 1103** with 64 Magicka. The old write-up dropped Spear for Psijic and put Destruction skills on a Restoration bar; that is not this kit. Guild **Psijic** stays only as a slotted tool (**Channeled Acceleration** for Major Force). Gear is 100% craftable (**Law of Julianos** + **Order's Wrath**) via account artisan **@masisi**.

## Roleplay: Emissary of the Solar Dawn

When Cyrodiil fell into chaos and imperial legitimacy crumbled, Pelatiah refused to bow to warlords or Daedric usurpers. Carrying the title **Emissary**, he traveled east and offered his oath to the Ebonheart Pact not out of fashion, but because Tamriel still needed an arbiter of divine light.

He is an Imperial magistrate who carries **both spear and sun**. He approaches combat with courtly discipline: he plants the sacred rune, then closes with **Puncturing Sweep** — the Aedric spear made Magicka — and finishes the condemned with **Radiant Glory**. He does not hide the spear behind a staff-only fantasy. The beam is the verdict; the jabs are the writ served in person.

Guild study of the Psijic Order taught him to **accelerate** a fight (Major Force), not to forget the spear. He remains a Templar first.

#### Solo combat tips

1. **Rune first.** Cast **Channeled Focus** before hard pulls. Standing in the rune is Major Resolve and the Magicka engine for jabs and the beam.
2. **Execute.** Below 50% Health, **Radiant Glory** is the verdict. The morph heals him from the damage he deals — stay in the channel unless the ground is lethal.
3. **Staff swap is loadout, not flavor.** Live Lightning-front / Inferno-back inverts Ancient Knowledge and leaves Wall of Storms on the wrong bar. Inferno front for the beam and jabs; Lightning back for the wall.
4. **Do not slot Crushing Shock.** The spammable is **Puncturing Sweep**. Dest filler is the wall and Susceptibility.

> [!NOTE]
> **Why not Night Mother's Gaze?** 5pc Major Breach is worth **0** while **Elemental Susceptibility** is already on the bar.
>
> **Why not New Moon Acolyte?** 5pc is +401 Spell Damage but **+5% ability cost** and a 9-trait gate — worse for a jab + beam Magicka kit.
>
> **Why not Shattered Fate 5pc?** Pen-only. Major Breach already covers a chunk of the 18,200 resistance cap (cap is general ESO knowledge — re-check after each patch). Crit from Julianos + Order's Wrath is the better crafted pair.
