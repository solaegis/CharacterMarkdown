## Roleplay: Pride Father

In Rimmen they still say his soft name under their breath — **Hya'cinthe** — the private word his mother kept for quiet rooms. On the streets, in the guild halls, and anywhere a younger cat needs a fixed star, he is **Pride Father**: not a blood-pride warlord, but **Abi** — the older brother the circle looks up to. He earned it the slow way. Showing up. Listening twice. Putting his body between trouble and people who trust him. Old soul. Measured. Patient. When strangers mean no harm, he is kind and curious; when the stack is threatened, the Beast answers with Apocrypha's green fire.

He ran with the Hollow Moon when secrets were a form of care — hiding found family from knives that wanted soft targets — not as a hired Assassin for gold. The Assassination title never fit the man who would rather teach a younger scribe how to stand than sell a throat. He left that kit behind. Violence for hire is a younger cat's mistake; protection is a father's craft.

Apocrypha did not make him cruel. It made him hungry. Crux, ink, and runes became a craft he can teach by example: how to hold a beam without panic, how to bargain with Dark Deal when the channel empties you, how to bind a Contingency sentence before the dig turns ugly. He is gay without spectacle — quiet devotion, scholarly intimacy, a hand on a shoulder that means *I have you*. He/him.

His primary life is patient and solitary: **lead-runner**, overland walker, antiquarian with a pocket watcher and a fox under the moon. Companion at his side. Rare pugs only — never a fixed co-op fantasy. Dual vocation: **Scribing** (he does not merely cast; he **binds ink to a page**) and **Antiquities** (green leads first, then deeper strata). The title on his papers is simple and final: **Pride Father**.

## Trinity configuration

### Subclassing decision (required)

Subclass unlocks at **Level 50** via Bahtra at-Hunding (**"A Study in Discipline"**). Hya is already 50. This build uses a legal **Arcanist Uber** (keep ≥1 native line; up to two foreign subclasses). See [docs/subclassing.md](../../../docs/subclassing.md).

**This build's decision:** **KEEP Herald of the Tome** and **KEEP Curative Runeforms**. **SUBCLASS Dark Magic** (Sorcerer) replacing **Soldier of Apocrypha**. Drop **Assassination** entirely (live leftover — not a target).

**Why not Apex Mystic (Herald + Dark Magic + Draconic)?** Pure beam DPS with Draconic breach is strong in groups, but Pride Father is a **true-solo** lead-runner. **Curative** owns self-heal, Gate mobility, and ink fantasy without needing a tank partner. **Dark Deal** keeps Fatecarver channels alive in digs and overland. Major Breach comes from **Elemental Drain** + **Crushing** glyph — Soldier's Runic Sunder is not required.

### Rotation

1. **Pre-pull (Ink Bargain):** Elemental Drain → Fulminating Rune → Evolving Runemend if low → Contingency (or Gate into position) → Dark Deal if mag is low.
2. **Swap to Father's Beam:** Elemental Blockade → Cephaliarch's Flail (build Crux) → weave light attacks → **Pragmatic Fatecarver** at 2–3 Crux.
3. **Sustain loop:** After channel, swap back → Dark Deal → refresh Drain / Fulminating / Contingency → beam again.
4. **Emergency:** Evolving Runemend; Gate out of AOE; potion; companion taunt if Bastian is out.
5. **Ultimate:** **The Languid Eye** from either bar on dig packs / world trash. (Alternate morph **The Unblinking Eye** is single-target — pick one morph account-wide, not both.)

### Passive skills

Prioritise: all **Herald of the Tome** combat passives; **Curative Runeforms** heal/cost passives; **Dark Magic** cost and Dark Deal passives; **Destruction Staff** Tri Focus / Ancient Knowledge / Destruction Expert; **Light Armor** (concentration / recovery); **Mages Guild** (Inner Light passive path — Major Savagery comes from Spell Power potion if Inner Light is not slotted); **Undaunted** Mettle; racial **Khajiit** Feline Ambush / robust.

### Scribing

Hya does not "cast a skill" in fiction — he **binds luminous ink to a prepared page**. Contingency is that sentence.

#### Beginner primer

1. **What it is:** A **Grimoire** (skill shell) + three **scripts** (Focus / Signature / Affix) bound with **Luminous Ink** at a Scribing altar.
2. **Unlock path:** Own the **Gold Road** chapter → complete the **Ulfsild / Scholarium** questline until the Scribing altar unlocks.
3. **Ink economy:** Luminous Ink drops from Scholarium content, dailies, and general play — check Journal / Scholarium vendors and solo-friendly overland sources. Exact drop tables change; farm what he can run alone.
4. **Script hunting:** Class scripts vs skill-line scripts. In the UI, read Focus (what the skill *does*), Signature (extra clause), Affix (usually a Major/Minor buff or utility).
5. **Anti-recipes for this build:** Do **not** scribe Affixes that grant **Major Brutality / Sorcery** or **Major Savagery / Prophecy** — **Recuperative Treatise** and Spell Power potions already cover those. Do not double Minor Force if another source already owns it.

#### This build's recipe card — Contingency

| Slot | Choice | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grimoire** | **Contingency** | Back-bar prepared buff / damage clause that fits ink fantasy |
| **Focus** | Damage or heal Focus (prefer Magic Damage for dig trash) | Keeps the page useful between beams |
| **Signature** | Magicka restore or critical Signature | Supports Fatecarver sustain / Khajiit crit |
| **Affix** | **Minor Force** | Crit damage without fighting Recuperative Treatise ownership |

Bind with Luminous Ink at the altar. Until scripts are collected, keep **Fleet-Footed Gate** in the Contingency slot.
