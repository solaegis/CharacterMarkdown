<a id="build-notes"></a>

## 📝 Build Notes

Masisi does not treat Summerset as a battlefield. To a Redguard artisan raised on caravan discipline, every ore vein is a ledger entry and every silk plant is cloth waiting for a buyer. He brings Ra Gada order to Tamriel’s mess: measure twice, harvest once, refine everything, and never leave a survey unread. His dragon fire lights the forge; his stamina keeps the mount between nodes. Guildmates ask for gold sets — he answers with stations, traits, and a motif book that will one day hold the account’s entire style library.
