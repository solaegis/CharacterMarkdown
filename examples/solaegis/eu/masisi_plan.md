## Resource strategy: "The Scavenger's Eye"

Minimize time at nodes; maximize yield and refine returns. At **CP 282** the Craft constellation has roughly **94** points and only **three** slottable stars — grow into the full farmer kit as CP rises.

### Live Craft CP (respec)

| **Star** | **Type** | **Live** | **Phase 0 target** | **Notes** |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| **Fortune's Favor** | Passive | 41 | 0 | Drop — gold later, not now |
| **Gilded Fingers** | Passive | 50 | 0 | Drop — gold later, not now |
| **Steed's Blessing** | Slottable | 0 | **50** | Between-node speed (slot) |
| **Master Gatherer** | Slottable | 0 | **30** (2 × 15) | Harvest speed; next stages toward **75** as Craft CP grows |
| **Gifted Rider** | Slottable | 0 | **10** (1 × 10) | Third slot — mount speed filler |
| *(unspent)* | — | — | **4** | Bank toward Master Gatherer stage 3 (needs 15) |

Craft budget at CP 282 is **94** points (91 spent + 3 free). **50 + 30 + 10 = 90**; leave **4** parked. Warfare’s **24** free points and Fitness’s **1** are optional for survivability; they are not the farmer’s priority.

### Grow-into farmer kit (as Craft CP increases)

1. **Master Gatherer (75)** — finish the slottable; primary harvest-speed star.
2. **Plentiful Harvest (50)** — passive; chance to double node yields.
3. **Meticulous Disassembly (50)** — passive; better refine returns on the mat flood.
4. Keep **Steed's Blessing (50)** slotted whenever farming.
5. Later: **Inspiration Boost (45)** while leveling Alchemy / Enchanting / Provisioning, then swap back to gold passives (**Gilded Fingers** / **Fortune's Favor**) once crafts are capped.

### Skill-point spend (20 available)

Priority order for the free skill points:

1. **Keen Eye** on Blacksmithing, Clothing, Woodworking, Jewelry (and Alchemy reagents when Alchemy ranks allow).
2. **Hirelings** — Miner, Outfitter, Lumberjack, then Enchanter / Forager as those lines level.
3. **Metallurgy / Stitching / Carpentry / Lapidary Research** — research-timer reductions toward 9/9 traits.
4. **Alliance War → Continuous Attack** morph of Rapid Maneuver (if not already morphed) for max mount speed.
5. Hold a few points for Alchemy / Enchanting / Provisioning passive ranks as those lines climb.

### Gathering routine

1. Eat food, pot **Essence of Speed**, mount **Psijic Escort Charger**, cast **Rapid Maneuver** (→ **Continuous Attack**).
2. Route surveys and dense node zones (Alinor hinterland, Glenumbra for Night's Silence mats, Wrothgar for Adept Rider when ready).
3. Harvest under **Master Gatherer**; refine with **Meticulous Disassembly** once funded.
4. Deposit mats to Crafting Bag; clear bank space before the next survey stack.

### Gathering tips

1. Pre-cast **Rapid Maneuver** before every mount stretch.

3. **Resolving Vigor** / **Coagulating Blood** if a pull goes wrong — then leave; do not stand and parse.
4. Use **Take Flight** to break bad packs in Craglorn / Imperial City node runs later.
