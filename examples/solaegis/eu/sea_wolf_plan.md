# Build Plan - Sea-Wolf: The Wolf of the Iliac Bay (Uber Warden Tank, Craftable)

> **Character profile:** [sea_wolf.md](sea_wolf.md). **Being recreated:** the current export is the Level 5 Nord Dragonknight (Ebonheart Pact) you're replacing. Re-export with `/cm` once the Nord **Warden** in the **Daggerfall Covenant** exists.

**Sea-Wolf** (saga name **Hastein**) is a Nord longship captain who raided the Breton coasts of the Iliac Bay, then took a Breton king's offer to raid his enemies instead. He's Tamriel's answer to **Hastein**, the Viking chief who raided the Frankish coast for thirty years and fought alongside the Breton king Salomon. The build is an **Uber-tier group tank** on a **Warden** base. The one native line it keeps is **Winter's Embrace**. At Level 50 it **subclasses Draconic Power** (replacing Animal Companions) and **Bone Tyrant** (replacing Green Balance). That combination won a scored search of all **1,330** three-line combinations (see [Trinity](#trinity-configuration)).

**Content target:** 4-player group dungeons (normal → veteran) and world bosses. **Gear rule:** **100% crafted** by @masisi, **12 pieces of Fellowship's Fortitude**, with no monster, trial or dungeon exceptions. Below Level 50 the advice is **best-now**, not a gap against the target.

## Roleplay: The Sea-Wolf

The Bretons of the Iliac Bay learned to watch the horizon for a striped sail. When it came, it came with forty oars, a wolf's head on the prow, and a Nord who didn't want their gold so much as their war. **Hastein**, called **Sea-Wolf**, raided their coasts for twenty summers. Then a Breton king made him a better offer: *raid my enemies instead*. He's been the Covenant's sea-wolf ever since. He's loyal to the contract, the crew and the shield wall, in that order.

He fights like the old **wolf-coats**, the Úlfheðnar: an animal's spirit at his shoulder and **Kyne's** storm at his back. **Winter's Embrace** is the northern sea he was born on and the goddess who rules it: ice armor, shields of frost, the sleet storm that breaks over the whole field. That power was born in him. The other two he took on the raids. **Draconic Power** is the dragon-prow of his *drakkar*: scales, the chains that drag the enemy onto his shields, dragon blood that closes his wounds. **Bone Tyrant** is the drowned crews who follow his wake: bone mail, and a grave-grip that gives his pack its courage.

A wolf hunts as a pack, and the pack lives while the strongest stands at the front. That's the whole of Hastein's philosophy, and the whole of his job. He wears the salt-stained garb of a sea-raider, the skald's brand on his face and Kyne's wind-caller marks on his skin. A wolf pup rides in his pack, and the ship's cat keeps the rats honest. Ashore he rides a Bleakrock snowdog, a wolf-hound bred on a northern island and carried south on the longship. He blows a dragonhorn to call his crew to the wall. When the enemy hesitates, he tells them to *come get some*.

### Name origin

**Hastein** (Old Norse *Hásteinn*) was one of the great Viking sea-kings of the 9th century. He raided the Loire and the Frankish coast, took service with the Breton king **Salomon** against the Franks, and (by legend) sailed into the Mediterranean. "Sea-Wolf" is the saga epithet for exactly that kind of raider. The in-game name is **Sea-Wolf**. Hastein is his saga name in roleplay.

## Trinity configuration

### Subclassing decision (required)

Subclassing unlocks at **Level 50** via Bahtra at-Hunding (**"A Study in Discipline"**). You must keep at least one native line (re-check in-game). This plan uses **two SUBCLASS rows**. Each one had to clearly beat the native line it replaces. See [docs/subclassing.md](../../../docs/subclassing.md) and [docs/dragonknight_u49.md](../../../docs/dragonknight_u49.md) (Draconic Power names after Update 49).

**How it was chosen:** every 3-line combination of the 21 class lines (1,330) was scored for tank needs:
- The weapons and Undaunted already cover the taunts, Major/Minor Breach, Major/Minor Maim and Minor Vulnerability.
- Duplicate buffs score zero.
- Class passives are scored too.

With 12 Fellowship supplying Major Protection, **Draconic Power + Winter's Embrace + Bone Tyrant** ranked **#1** (61.0). A second search added scribed skills, passive-granted buffs and free competition for the Undaunted slots. The trio still ranked #1, **tied** with Draconic Power + Green Balance + Bone Tyrant (see the [Maturation note](#subclassing-decision-required)). Across 1,500 weight tests (±50%), it was #1 in 22% and top 3 in 54%, the highest top-3 rate. No other configuration scored higher. The end-state stats are the same whichever class owns the native line. **Warden** is the base because:
- **Winter's Embrace**, the build's most valuable line, is already fully ranked when subclassing opens at 50.
- It's the best roleplay fit (Kyne's storm, the wolf-coats).

**Why each native line goes or stays** (buff sources from `data/sets/buffs.json`):

| **Native line** | **Decision** | **Proof** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

> **Re-test at Level 50: Maturation decides the third line.** Green Balance's **Maturation** passive is the build's only practical source of **Minor Toughness** (+10% Max Health, about +4,000). No set or scribed skill gives it; `buffs.json`'s only other source is an Arcanist-only effect. A second search, which added scribed skills and passive-granted buffs, found a **tie**: **Draconic Power + Green Balance + Bone Tyrant** scored the same as this plan's trio (61.0 each) and won slightly more of the ±50% weight tests (25% vs 22%). > - **Maturation self-applies:** the tie is real. Either trio is fine; this plan's version keeps Winter's Embrace, which is already ranked.
> - **It only affects allies:** Green Balance has no tank value, so subclass it away as planned.

### Rotation and combat tips

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    pre["Pre-pull: Ice Fortress (front) · Shimmering Shield (back)"] --> pull["Front: Chains of Devastation the pack · Pierce Armor the boss · Empowering Grasp the pack"]
    pull --> swap["Back: Frost Clench a second target · Inner Beast · Banner Bearer on the stack"]
    swap --> hold["Hold block · light-attack between casts"]
    hold --> refresh{"What's about to drop?"}
    refresh -->|Ice Fortress / Grasp| front["Front: refresh"]
    refresh -->|Shimmering Shield| back["Back: refresh (keeps Major Heroism rolling)"]
    refresh -->|Taunt| taunt["Pierce Armor (front) / Frost Clench (back)"]
    front --> hold
    back --> hold
    taunt --> hold
    hold --> spike["Spike: Blood of the Elder Dragon · Bone Goliath · Sleet Storm for the group"]
    spike --> hold
```

1. **Taunt timer first.** Taunts last 15 s. Re-taunt at about 12 s, and don't re-taunt what you already hold.
2. **Ultimate is your group job.** Shimmering Shield (Major Heroism) and Banner Bearer (Minor Heroism) feed your ultimates. Plant the banner where the group stacks, keep the shield up, and spend Sleet Storm or Aggressive Horn on the group's burn windows.
3. **Grasp on pull.** Empowering Grasp as the pack lands gives Major Courage just as the DPS start their AoE.
4. **Block is the default state.** Burnished Scales rewards blocking. Drop block only to cast, light-attack or bash an interruptible cast.
5. **Face the boss away from the group** and stop moving once the pack is stacked.

## Gear and crafting: "The Fellowship Hauberk"

> [!NOTE]
> **Resistance cap check (re-check after patches):** mitigation is about 1% per 660 resistance, capping at 50% (**~33,000**). Ice Fortress (+5,948 Major Resolve), Bone Armor (+2,974 Minor Resolve), the set (+7,425), heavy armor, shield, Nord Rugged and Fortified CP will likely pass it. > - **Above ~33,000:** swap Bone Armor for Bone Totem or Frozen Device, and change the sword trait from Defending to **Decisive**.
> - **Below ~31,000:** change one ring from Healthy to **Protective** (+1,190).
>
> These caps are general ESO knowledge, not yet in the verified dataset.
